About Us

Democratizing Chemical Sensing Through Innovation

ScensAI is a University of Aberdeen spin-out on a mission to make chemical sensing accessible to everyone. Traditional chemical analysis requires expensive laboratory equipment and expert knowledge. We're changing that.

Our pocket Electronic Nose (ENose) leverages multiple sensor types combined with sophisticated AI models and chemical modelling to deliver laboratory-grade analysis in a portable, user-friendly device. From environmental monitoring to food safety, industrial applications to personal health, we're opening up a world of possibilities.

Learn more about our Electronic Nose technology and how it can help you.

An Electronic Nose (ENose) is a device that mimics the human sense of smell by using an array of chemical sensors combined with pattern recognition algorithms. Our ENose combines multiple sensor types with AI and chemical modelling to identify and quantify chemicals in the environment.
Our ENose has diverse applications including environmental monitoring, food quality and safety testing, industrial process control, medical diagnostics, agriculture, and workplace safety. The portable design makes it suitable for both field and laboratory use across multiple industries.
Our AI models are trained on vast datasets of chemical signatures, allowing the ENose to recognize complex patterns and identify chemicals with high accuracy. The AI works in tandem with chemical modelling to provide both identification and quantification of detected substances.
We are currently in the development and testing phase. For updates on availability and potential partnership opportunities, please get in touch via our contact section. We're actively seeking collaborators and early adopters.
